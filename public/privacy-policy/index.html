1. Who we are

ClinicConnect ("we", "us", "our") provides a WhatsApp-based appointment booking, reminder, and medical report delivery system that clinics ("Clinics") use to communicate with their patients ("you", "patients"). Each Clinic that uses ClinicConnect is its own independent healthcare provider — ClinicConnect itself is not a hospital, clinic, doctor, or healthcare provider. We are a technology platform that clinics use to talk to their patients; we do not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment, and we are not responsible for the clinical decisions made by any doctor or clinic using our platform.

This policy explains what information passes through ClinicConnect when you message a clinic that uses our platform, and what we do with it.

2. What data we collect

We only collect what's needed to run your appointments and keep the clinic's records straight:

CategoryExamplesWhere it comes from
Contact details Your WhatsApp number, and the name you give us Directly from you, when you message the clinic
Appointment details Doctor chosen, date, time, token number, patient age, and the reason you give for the visit Directly from you, during the booking conversation
Health-adjacent documents Medical reports or documents the clinic sends you (e.g. a PDF report) From clinic staff, for delivery to you — we don't read, analyze, or act on the contents
Message history The text of messages you and the clinic exchange, and delivery/read status Automatically logged as messages are sent and received, for support and audit purposes
Technical identifiers The WhatsApp ID Meta assigns to your number, message IDs, timestamps Provided automatically by Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform

We do not ask for or store payment details, government ID numbers, or precise location data. We do not access anything on your phone beyond the messages you send us on WhatsApp.

3. How we use your data

  • To book, reschedule, and confirm your appointments
  • To send you appointment reminders and status updates
  • To deliver medical reports or documents your clinic sends you
  • To let clinic staff (receptionists, doctors) manage their own day-to-day schedule
  • To keep an audit trail of what was sent and received, in case of a dispute or a delivery problem
  • To maintain and improve the reliability of the platform (e.g. debugging a failed message)
  • To meet legal or regulatory obligations we or the clinic are subject to

We do not use your data to build an advertising profile, sell it to third parties, or share it with any clinic other than the one you're actually messaging.

4. Who we share it with

  • The clinic you're messaging — their doctors and receptionists can see your appointments, messages, and reports so they can treat you. They cannot see any other clinic's patients.
  • Meta / WhatsApp — as the messaging channel itself (see the next section).
  • Infrastructure providers who host our database, servers, and message queue — they process data on our instructions and don't use it for their own purposes.
  • Law enforcement or regulators, only if we're legally required to.

We never sell personal data, and we never share it with advertisers.

5. About WhatsApp & Meta

Every message you send us, and every message we send you, travels through Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform (Cloud API). This means:

  • Meta processes your messages as our service provider, strictly to deliver them — not to target you with ads. WhatsApp messages are not used to inform the ads you see on Facebook or Instagram.
  • Meta's own infrastructure retains a copy of message content for a short window (currently up to 30 days) to support delivery and retransmission, independent of how long we keep our own copy.
  • WhatsApp's own Privacy Policy governs how Meta itself handles data on its side of the platform.

You can ask us (or your clinic) to delete your data sooner — see "Your rights" below. We may need to keep some records longer than requested if the clinic is legally required to retain patient records for a minimum period.

7. How we protect your data

  • Messages are encrypted in transit between you, WhatsApp, and our servers.
  • Access to patient data is restricted to the specific clinic's own staff — not visible to other clinics on the platform.
  • We apply reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to prevent unauthorized access, but no system can be guaranteed 100% secure, and we ask that clinic staff also handle patient information responsibly on their end.

8. Your rights

You can, at any time, ask us or your clinic to:

  • Access a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate information (e.g. a misspelled name)
  • Delete your data, subject to any legal retention requirements your clinic has
  • Withdraw consent to further communication — simply tell the clinic, or stop responding; you can also message [STOP / your chosen opt-out keyword] to pause non-essential messages

To exercise any of these, contact us using the details in "Contact us" below, or speak to your clinic directly.

9. Appointments booked for children or dependents

Patients of any age may receive care through a clinic using this platform. When an appointment is booked for a child or dependent, we assume the person messaging us is a parent, guardian, or otherwise authorized to act on that patient's behalf, and that they have the authority to share the information provided.
